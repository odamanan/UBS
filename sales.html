How Sales Works

This page reflects the Sprint 2 sales flow described in your backlog.

Story Flow

1. Member Posts Listing

A registered member adds a title, description, price, and condition for an item they want to sell, lend, or exchange.

2. Other Members Browse Listings

Students can look through posted items by category and see whether an item is for sale, lending, or exchange.

3. User Initiates Transaction

When a member finds an item they want, they can begin a transaction directly from the listing card.

4. Payment Story Connects Next

For purchases, clicking Buy Item now takes the user directly to the Payments page with the item pre-selected.
